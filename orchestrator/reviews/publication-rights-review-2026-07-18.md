## Decision

The current implementation may proceed into QA under a narrow non-monetized fan-tool scope:

- free, with no ads, affiliate links, payments or account system;
- independent and unofficial disclosures in the footer, FAQ, About and Disclaimer;
- no official game artwork, screenshots, audio, logos or copied competitor presentation;
- factual, versioned outputs with visible verification status and no accuracy guarantee;
- third-party sites used for read-only corroboration only, with no stored page snapshot or redistributed bulk export;
- production data remains fail-closed until Owner publication review.

Pocketpair's derivative-work guideline permits fan works subject to restrictions, but it does not expressly approve factual calculator databases. It also prohibits highly commercial or profit-oriented use and content that could be mistaken for official products. This supports the current conservative MVP boundary but is not an affirmative database license.
