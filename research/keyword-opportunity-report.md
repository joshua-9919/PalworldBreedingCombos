# Palworld Breeding Keyword Opportunity Report

Date: 2026-07-17
Market: US / English
Domain: `palworldbreedingcombos.com`
Decision: **BUILD_NOW**

## Executive conclusion

`palworld breeding` is the durable parent topic. `palworld breeding calculator` is the highest-intent tool query, while `palworld breeding combos` and entity-specific pages provide the scalable long-tail surface. Palworld 1.0 created a freshness reset because the breeding roster/table changed; however, multiple exact-match and established competitors have already published updated calculators. A new site can enter only by combining visibly versioned data with owned-Pal planning, shortest feasible chains, and entity-level answer pages.

### Tier C — programmatic entity demand

Canonical pattern: `/breeding/{pal-slug}/`

Each page must answer:

- How to breed `{Pal}` in Palworld 1.0.
- Every direct parent pair that produces `{Pal}`.
- Practical/easy pairs before late-game or legendary pairs.
- Shortest chains from common starting Pals.
- `{Pal}` as a parent: notable offspring.
- Version, data source and last verified date.

Prioritize autocomplete/community-recognized entities such as Anubis, Jetragon, Shadowbeak, Grizzbolt, Relaxaurus, Jormuntide Ignis, Yakumo and new 1.0 Pals after validation.

Interpretation: the topic has both immediate freshness demand and durable task/entity demand. The year/version belongs in copy and data badges, not in the domain or canonical route.

## SERP structure

### Established competitors

- `palworld.gg`: calculator plus broad database, map and game tools; strong product breadth and existing recognition.
- PalDB: database-first destination with breeding tools and community familiarity.
- Game8: high-authority editorial guide/calculator surface.
- HostHavoc and other established gaming/service sites: updated 1.0 calculator pages.

### Fast 1.0 entrants

Recent SERPs contain multiple newly published exact-match/descriptive sites, including `palworldbreedingcalculator.best`, `palworldbreeding.org`, `palworldbreedcalculator.com`, `palworld-breeding.com` and planner-style brands. Indexation of fresh small sites proves a temporary entry window, while their density raises the minimum viable quality.

### Community-described gaps

- Use only Pals the player already owns.
- Exclude legendary, unavailable or ultra-late-game parents.
- Find the shortest feasible multi-step chain.
- Explain changed/uncertain 1.0 results and publish raw/versioned data notes.
- Plan passives, IVs and mutation probability instead of only species output.

## Competitor minimum capability contract

The MVP must not launch without:

1. Parents → child.
2. Target child → all parent pairs.
3. One parent → possible partners/results.
4. Search across the validated 1.0 roster and aliases.
5. Owned-Pals filter and practical route constraints.
6. Shortest chain to a target.
7. Visible version, dataset hash/version, updated date and source policy.
8. Mobile usability, shareable states/URLs, no-login core usage.
9. Indexable combo/entity pages that contain useful answers without client-only blank HTML.

## Differentiation

Positioning:

> The Palworld 1.0 breeding planner for combos you can actually make.

Product promise:

- Start from what is in the player's Palbox.
- Prefer reachable parents over theoretically valid but impractical pairs.
- Show direct results and shortest chains in one workflow.
- Never hide data freshness or uncertainty.

## Domain decision

`palworldbreedingcombos.com` aligns with the major `palworld breeding combos` cluster and leaves room for calculator, guide and planner features. It is the only canonical production domain. Defensive domains are unnecessary for MVP.

Risk: `Palworld` is a brand-bearing term. The site must state that it is an independent, unofficial fan-made tool; it must not copy official trade dress or imply endorsement.
