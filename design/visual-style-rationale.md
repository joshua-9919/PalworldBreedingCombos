# Visual Style Rationale

Status: SELECTED
Project: Palworld Breeding Combos

## Directions compared

### A. Generic dark gaming dashboard

- Neon purple/blue gradient, glass cards, dense sidebar.
- Familiar to gaming users, but highly interchangeable with AI/SaaS templates.
- Weak fit for long-form guides and trust/version metadata.
- Rejected: too generic and visually close to common generated dashboards.

### B. Bright creature encyclopedia

- White canvas, colorful collectible cards, large character artwork.
- Strong browsing affordance, but depends on copyrighted character assets and makes the calculator feel secondary.
- Rejected for MVP: asset-rights risk and weak alignment with serious route planning.

### C. Night field journal + genetics workbench — selected

- Deep ink background, parchment panels, warm egg/gold action color, restrained elemental accents.
- Breeding nodes and connecting lines become the primary original visual language.
- Supports both calculator density and readable editorial sections.
- Feels game-adjacent without copying the official logo, UI or character art.
- Works with original CSS shapes, text and glyphs, keeping MVP asset-safe and fast.

## Selected rationale

The primary user arrives with a concrete task and often distrusts old data. The interface must therefore look like a working research instrument rather than a promotional landing page. The main canvas places selection controls, result lineage and dataset metadata together. Editorial copy moves into lighter parchment-like sections below, preserving SEO content without weakening the tool-first hierarchy.

## Originality constraints

- No official logo, game screenshots, Pal portraits or copied elemental icons.
- No purple gradient hero, floating glass orbs or centered three-card SaaS layout.
- Use custom egg/node marks, monoline links and stamped data-status labels.
- Product name uses standard text typography, not a recreation of Palworld branding.
