Quick answers

Palworld breeding calculator FAQ

How does Palworld breeding work?

Most offspring are determined from the parents’ hidden breeding values, while special combinations can override the normal calculation. The exact rules and roster must match the game and dataset version shown with the result.

Can I find parents for a specific Pal?

Yes. Choose Target → Parents, select the Pal you want, and compare its validated direct parent pairs.

Is this an official Palworld tool?

No. Palworld Breeding Combos is an independent, unofficial fan-made tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pocketpair.
